# Code of Conduct

Cirruscope is a small, friendly project. The whole standard is: **be respectful.**

That means assuming good faith, keeping discussion about the work rather than the person, and accepting that decisions won't always go your way — see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) for how and why those calls get made. Disagreement is welcome; hostility, harassment, entitlement, and personal attacks are not.

As the maintainer, I moderate the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, and discussions). I may edit, lock, or close any thread that turns hostile or unproductive, and remove anyone who repeatedly behaves that way. This isn't personal — it's how I keep the project a place I still enjoy working on.

If you experience or witness behavior that crosses the line, or you'd rather raise something privately, email **contact@cirruscope.app**.
